# Project Constitution

## Mission
Create a useful, free, downloadable AI course and website that teaches ordinary people how to understand, communicate with, evaluate, and use AI from beginner level through advanced practical workflows.

## Human authority
AI must never replace the owner's authority. It may inspect, suggest, draft, test, and prepare. Publishing, deleting, spending, changing access, connecting sensitive data, or making irreversible changes requires explicit human approval.

## Product principles
1. Useful before impressive.
2. Advanced enough to remain valuable beyond AI basics.
3. Clear examples users can copy and adapt.
4. Practical connected-app and agent workflows.
5. Explicit evaluation, limitations, privacy, and safety guidance.
6. Mobile-first and easy to navigate.
7. Course remains free and downloadable unless the owner explicitly changes that policy.
8. Avoid unnecessary complexity, filler, and repetitive explanations.
9. Preserve the live site's working download flow during migration.
10. Prefer portable standards and version-controlled source over platform lock-in.
